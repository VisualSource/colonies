# MineColonies parity checklist

What MineCP has compared with MineColonies (`version/main`), and what is left. `[x]` done, `[~]` partly done (gaps listed under it), `[ ]` not started, ~~crossed out~~ skipped. Add notes under any item.

## Core colony

- [~] Town Hall: founds the colony, levels gate other huts' levels, citizen list
  - Breaking the town hall disbands the colony, so only its owner may (ours: the mod lets BREAK_HUTS decide)
  - No colony events or statistics (advancements are listed below)
- [~] Chunk claims (`core/util/ChunkDataHelper.java`, each building's `getClaimRadius`): the claimed chunks are the colony's border. Numbers are the mod's server config defaults, set in `config.yml` (`claims`)
  - Founding claims every chunk within 4 of the town hall's chunk (`initialColonySize` 4)
  - A new town hall must be at least 128 blocks (`max(minColonyDistance 8, initialColonySize 4)` chunks) from every other town hall, and none of its first chunks may be claimed already (the mod's `isFarEnoughFromColonies`)
  - No claim reaches more than 20 chunks from the town hall's chunk (`maxColonySize` 20)
  - Each hut claims a square around its own chunk by its level, plus every chunk its built building covers; a removed hut gives them back. Radius: most huts 0 at level 0, 1 at levels 1-3, 2 at levels 4-5; town hall 0, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5; fields none
  - Any hut but a town hall goes inside a colony's border, its whole building too (checked for its level 1 building; an upgrade is refused if its next level would reach into another colony's chunks, while unclaimed land is fine), and joins the colony that owns that ground, so an officer can place huts for someone else's colony (the mod's `SurvivalHandler` and `AbstractBlockHut.canPaste`)
  - Claims are worked out from the huts, not stored. As in the mod, a chunk stays with the colony that claimed it first, and a colony's first chunks are never given up. After a restart, though, chunks two colonies' buildings both claim go to the older colony (we don't save who claimed first)
  - Build Goggles (the mod's recipe; a dyed leather helmet with a tag stands in for its model): while worn, colony borders within 4 chunks stand as see-through glass panes 40 blocks tall around you, lime for colonies you belong to and red for others, only for you; they follow as you walk (the mod draws the border with its own client renderer)
  - Not done: the goggles' other uses in the mod (showing buildings' outlines)
  - Guard tower, barracks and gate house radii wait for those buildings (guard tower 2, 3, 3, 4, 5; barracks 3, or 2 while a tower is below level 4; gate house 1, 1, 2)
  - Not done: keeping the colony's chunks loaded (`forceloadcolony`); claiming chunks with commands
- [~] Permissions (`core/colony/permissions/Permissions.java`, `ColonyPermissionEventHandler`): the mod's five ranks (owner, officer, friend, neutral for everyone else, hostile) with its default actions; each colony can change what each rank may do. The owner edits members and ranks from the town hall menu ("Permissions") or with `/colony member add|remove` and `/colony members`
  - Inside the border, other players' block breaking and placing, lighting fires, using doors and gates, containers and other blocks, buckets, dropping and picking up items, throwing potions, shooting arrows, using and attacking entities (hostile mobs excepted) and breaking item frames and armor stands follow their rank (either hand); hut menus need ACCESS_HUTS, their buttons MANAGE_HUTS, placing huts PLACE_HUTS (build tool: MANAGE_HUTS), breaking them BREAK_HUTS; graves follow OPEN_CONTAINER and BREAK_BLOCKS wherever they are
  - Pistons, flowing water and lava, and fire (spreading, lighting or burning) can't cross from one owner's land into another colony's chunks, so nobody can push, pull, flood or burn in from just outside the border (ours: the mod relies on its event handler for players only)
  - Attacking a citizen while standing in the colony needs ATTACK_CITIZEN, and so does attacking one that stands in the colony from outside it (ours: the mod judges only where the attacker stands, so a neutral player one block outside can hit citizens); and a player's hit of more than 1 needs HURT_CITIZEN (by default only hostile has it, so even the owner deals 1), as in the mod
  - Colony messages go to every online member with RECEIVE_MESSAGES (owner and officers by default)
  - A server operator in creative mode gets the mod's OP actions on top of their rank (the mod: permission level 2; ours: `minecp.admin`)
  - Explosions inside the border: `config.yml` `protection.explosions`, the mod's four choices; defaults to vanilla (everything is damaged) rather than the mod's "entities"; hut blocks and graves are never damaged
  - Neutral can never be given EDIT_PERMISSIONS, and a rank can't take EDIT_PERMISSIONS away from itself, as in the mod
  - `/colony members` shows the member list only to members (and admins)
  - Only the five built-in ranks: no custom ranks yet
  - Players are added by name only if they have played on the server (the mod: its profile cache)
  - Not done: the scan tool, guard rally, map, teleport and explosion actions (their features don't exist yet), free blocks and positions (the mod's lists of blocks anyone may use), PvP mode, the "abandoned colony" rules, denial messages to the owner
- [x] Supply camp and supply ship starts (`ItemSupplyCampDeployer`, `ItemSupplyChestDeployer`, `SuppliesHandler`), the mod's numbers unless noted: the Supply Camp (paper stands in for its model; the mod's recipe from 5 chests: plain or trapped chests, not hut items or racks, which are chests too) and Supply Ship (paper too; from 5 plain boats of any kind, not another Supply Ship) stack to 1. Right-click (main hand; from the off hand it does nothing): pick a style, then the build tool's preview opens 10 blocks ahead, on the highest block there (the camp on the ground, the ship's keel 3 below the water's surface; the mod starts one block above the clicked block), moved, turned and confirmed the same way. Confirming checks the mod's rules, a third of the footprint allowed to fail: the camp stands on solid ground with nothing on it; the ship's hull is in water for its 3 lowest layers with nothing in its way for 7 more. The levels (1 ground, 3 water) are the mod's defaults, which every style's supply blueprints use, so they aren't read from the blueprints' tag. Overworld only. It is placed at once with everything in it (vanilla's structure placement), the item used up and the placement recorded, once per player; in creative the item is still needed and used up, but earlier placements don't block (all as the mod's). It gives the MineColonies root advancement
  - Ours, stricter: no part may be inside a colony where the player can't place blocks (the mod counts those spots among the third); no chest or other container may be in the way (its items would be lost); the ship's 3 lowest layers must be mostly water (the mod checks only the hull's own blocks there, since the blueprints mark nearly all the rest as fluid, so a ship could sit on dry ground)
  - Its barrel holds the mod's starting kit from the blueprint: a Town Hall and a Build Tool (the converter keeps the mod's two items as tagged stand-ins that the placement swaps for ours), paper, logs, stone, cobblestone, food, potions and stone tools. The cavern camp and ship have no kit in their barrels: the player is given the Town Hall and Build Tool instead (ours)
  - Not done: the mod's story screen before the first placement, the Nether ship, free deployers in vanilla loot chests
- [ ] Colony flag and banner
- [ ] Colony connections (`core/colony/managers/ColonyConnectionManager.java`, `api/colony/connections/`): colony signs and gate houses link two colonies, with a diplomacy status of allies, neutral or hostile
- [ ] Commands: the mod's `/mc colony ...` and `/mc citizens ...` (MineCP's `/colony` has give-item, `build`, `info` and `movement` subcommands)
- [~] Advancements (the mod's `data/minecolonies/advancements/`): its MineColonies, production and military tabs, with the mod's tree, frames, triggers and vanilla icons (our own icons where the mod shows its hut blocks), in our own words. Loaded by the plugin at startup (`Advancements`), so no data pack is needed; a `/minecraft:reload` drops them until the next restart. Bukkit saves them in the world's `bukkit` data pack: when a MineCP update changes one, all of them are replaced at the next start (one data reload)
  - Each is awarded when it happens to every online player the colony sends messages to (RECEIVE_MESSAGES), as the mod does: placing a supply camp or ship (the tab's root, with the mod's quiet copy in vanilla's Story tab; awarded to the one who placed it), placing the town hall, asking for the Builder's Hut, finishing huts and levels, 5/10/25/50/75 citizens, a citizen's first meal, teaching torches or a wooden door; placing a postbox and a stash (the mod's two placed_block criteria, each awarded to the one who placed it)
  - Also: the miner's shaft within 8 blocks of the world's bottom (deep mine), a farmer with 5 fields (max fields), and the military tab: finishing a Guard Tower (its root, with stone bricks behind it as the mod's) and 8, 30 and 80 guards (ours: counted each colony tick, where the mod counts when one is hired)
  - Left out until their features exist: mystical site, the undertaker's totem, the hut guide, fulfilling a request from the request screen, enchanter, florist, library. Finishing a Crusher or a Sifter is in the production tab. Rotten flesh can't happen: it's on the mod's list of foods citizens refuse, as on ours. The mod has no research advancements

## Citizens

- [~] Citizens: names, skins, wandering, homes, jobs
  - Right-clicking a citizen or visitor stops it for 3 seconds, facing the player, with its work paused (the mod's `EntityCitizen.mobInteract`); not while it is on a ladder or running from danger
  - A hut takes a worker once level 1 is built, as in the mod (`IBuilding.canAssignCitizens`); only the Builder's Hut hires at level 0, so its builder can build it
  - Skills (`Skill`, `CitizenSkillHandler`, `CitizenExperienceHandler`, `ExperienceUtils`), the mod's numbers unless noted: eleven skills (athletics, dexterity, strength, agility, stamina, mana, adaptability, focus, creativity, knowledge, intelligence), levels 1 to 99. The next level takes 1 + 5 x level + 0.005 x level³ experience. Each job has the mod's main and second skill (a guard's by its type); work gives the mod's experience at the mod's moments (0.05 per block broken or placed, 1 per tree felled or plantation harvest, 0.5 per farm crop, 2 per meal served, treatment or study, 1 per composter barrel worked, 5 per furnace a crafter empties (the cook's 2), half a craft's count, 1.5 per delivery, 0.5 per herding action, 5 per hive emptied, 15 per monster a guard fought dying, 8 per build finished, and so on). It counts times 1 + (its work building's level + its home's) / 10, plus its intelligence in %, times 1 + the levelling research; none while starving. The main skill gets it all, the second half; the main's complementary skill gets 10% and its adverse one loses 10%, the second's 5%. A skill gains nothing once at 10 levels per home level plus 10 (homeless: 10; a tavern, whose top level is 3: 40), with no limit in a home at its top level. A level up shows with a sound and sparkles
  - Intelligence grows at the School and Library (School and Library)
  - Starting skills, as the mod's: a new citizen's are rolled from 1 up to twice the colony's happiness (at least 5 for the 4 starting citizens); a child's up to the colony's happiness, plus 25 to 49 points shared by its role models' levels (ours: two citizens at random, as MineCP has no families); a visitor's up to 15 + rarity² / 2. Ours: a citizen brought back by the undertaker rolls them again (the mod keeps them). The citizen menu shows its job's two skills with their progress, and the rest; a visitor's menu shows its skills
  - Experience orbs (the mod's gatherXp, applyMending and dropExperience): once a second a citizen takes the orbs near it (within 2 blocks, 4 while pulling some or 1 time in 5; at least 5 ticks old): one within a block, or after 5 pulls, is absorbed, the rest pulled in. Absorbed experience first mends its Mending items, 2 a point of durability (ours: any in its inventory; the mod's what it holds and wears), then goes into its job's skills as work experience; the jobless take orbs but learn nothing, as the mod's. The fisherman's catches drop an orb of 2 each at it (the mod's bobber). A citizen killed with a player's hit in the last 5 s drops its experience towards next levels as orbs, with mob loot on
  - What skills change, as the mod's (each under its job): guards' health, attack speed, naps, aim, range, arrow damage and potion length; the beekeeper's pace and its bees' anger; the composter's barrels and yields; the healer's outright cures; the sifter's and crusher's pace, the crusher's sickness; crafters' hits per craft; the farmer's pace; the fisherman's failures and lure speed; herders' butchering; the undertaker's work and its chance to bring someone back. Also break and place times (miner, lumberjack, builder, concrete mixer: the mod's mining and place times) and furnaces: a crafter smelts in at most its main skill / 10 + 1 of them at once, and a worker's skill cooks its furnaces faster (a crafter's second skill / 10 x 2 extra ticks a second; the cook's and the Smeltery's main, and the Smeltery has no furnace limit, as the mod's; ours: stopping one tick short of done, so vanilla finishes the item).
  - Each citizen has its own inventory (the mod's citizen inventory: 27 slots, more with research), opened from its menu's Inventory button (MANAGE_HUTS, as the mod's): it eats food from there first, gets well with cure items there, and it goes into its grave when it dies
  - Workers work from it, as the mod's: their tools, the materials they carry (a builder's blocks and racks, a miner's stock), what they gather (harvests, catches, loot, a grave's contents) and a courier's deliveries are all in it, so a player can hand a worker what it needs, or take something out. It holds what its slots hold: what doesn't fit drops at the worker's feet, the miner and farmer go back to their hut when it is full, the fisherman too, and a courier takes only what fits and delivers all it carries, tools and food included. Handing in at its hut, a worker keeps one tool of each kind (more, from a grave or caught as junk, go in), a stack of food to eat (ours: one stack, so a farmer's carrots still go in) and what its job keeps on hand (a miner's cobblestone, fill block and ladders and what its work needs; a herder's breeding item, buckets and bowls; a healer's cure items). A citizen keeps its inventory when it changes job or is fired, as in the mod. Not in it: a knight's armor and shield, kept by its tower, and food a cook is carrying to a customer
  - No chat interactions or requests above citizens' heads; reasons are shown in the hut menus instead
  - Movement: our own route search (`Route`, from the mod's pathfinding rules and costs: ladders up and down, drops up to 10 blocks at dy³, jumps +2, doors and fence gates +2 (opened on the way and closed behind), path blocks cheaper, wading in shallow water, the mod's node limit by distance), followed by steering the legs mob directly; ladders are climbed and slid with vanilla physics; when stuck (3 seconds without getting half a block further across the ground; hopping in place doesn't count) it searches again, then teleports 5 steps ahead (the mod's order); doors (as they are when open) and open trapdoors are thin panels on one side of their block, read from their collision shape: routes pass along a panel, never step, jump or cut a corner across one (ours: the mod's pathfinding treats them as passable or not); `/colony movement` lists each citizen's teleports, stuck events and search times
    - The search runs on the main thread (like vanilla's), not on a worker thread as in the mod: a warm 70-block detour takes ~7 ms, short trips ~1 ms
    - No swimming or diving (citizens wade only), no rails, no vines unless tagged climbable
    - Heuristic weight fixed at 2 (the mod starts at 2 and tunes it per citizen); path blocks cost 1/2 (the mod: 1/6)
    - Teleports near the goal when nothing reaches it (the mod waits at least 2 minutes first)
    - Moves diagonally on level ground, at √2 the cost of a straight step; a step through water costs 4 (not checked against the mod)

- [~] Colony growth: a new colony starts empty; its first 4 citizens (the mod's `initialCitizenAmount`) move in one at a time at the town hall on the mod's timer (`CitizenManager.onColonyTick`: checked every 500 ticks, the countdown drops by 500 plus 60 per town hall level, 30 s before the first and 60 s after each; about 50 s for the first, then about 75 s apart), half and half female and male, and are replaced the same way if they die. After them the colony grows only as in the mod: by children (below) and by recruiting the taverns' visitors (Visitors and the tavern). Housing is the mod's `getMaxCitizens`: the beds of built houses (1 per level) and taverns (4), and the guards and trainees who live at work, at least 1 and at most 25 (the mod's limit before research); the starting citizens move in even without beds
  - The Town Hall's "citizens move in" setting turns this off, and hiring modes choose who works and lives where (Hut settings); research raises the 25 limit (Outpost 50, Hamlet 100, Village 150, City 500, never past the mod's default server limit of 250)
- [x] Tool tiers limited by the worker's own hut level (`IBuilding.getMaxEquipmentLevel`, `ItemStackUtils.verifyEquipmentLevel`): at most stone up to hut level 1, then the hut's level (iron 2, diamond 3, netherite 4), anything at level 5; a tool's tier is wood/gold 0, stone 1, iron 2, diamond 3, netherite 4, plus its highest enchantment level minus 1. Better tools stay in the chest (and couriers may take them away); requests say the best tier allowed; of the tools that will do, the lowest tier is taken, as in the mod
  - Copper tools (newer than the mod) count as stone
  - The same rule covers every worker's tools and the fisherman's rod; not tied to research or the builder
- [~] Hunger and food (saturation numbers from the mod)
  - Work adds to it as the mod's: each action (a block dug or placed, a finished craft, a catch, a delivery, a swing at an animal) 0.2, and 0.02 for a crush or every 25 of vanilla's walk distance (about 42 blocks), plus a full action when the crusher's daily batch is done; added to the next drop but at most half of it. Knights and researchers don't count actions, as the mod's guards and researchers. Children drop half as fast. Ours: a worker's swing is mostly its action, so some jobs count a little differently from the mod's own AI
  - Saturation only drops in daytime and not while asleep, as in the mod (`EntityCitizen.decreaseIdleSaturation`)
  - No food quality tied to house level
  - No food variety (diet diversity)
  - Saturation sets how fast health comes back (see Health)
- [~] Day/night routine (`CitizenAI`, `EntityAISleep`, `CitizenSleepHandler`): from day time 10600 citizens leave for home when the walk (6 ticks a block, height counted 1.5x, the miner's climb added) would get them there by nightfall at 12600; they sleep in a house bed and get up at dawn; a colony day counter goes up each morning; in rain only the cook and level 5 huts' workers work
  - Beds are found by scanning the home's building (5 blocks below its hut block to 12 above: every blueprint's beds; the mod registers them), nearest the hut block first
  - Homeless citizens wait within 16 blocks of the town hall (the mod sends them to a tavern if there is one); a tavern's residents sleep in its building's beds, like a house's
  - No "all citizens are asleep" message, sleeping particles, off-to-bed sounds, or "home too far" complaint
  - Research lets them work 1 or 2 hours later, and in the rain (Research)
  - Citizens other than guards go home during a raid; a sick citizen in a hospital bed stays there through raids and nights, and otherwise sickness comes after the night and before meals and work, as the mod's; mourners skip work but sleep as usual
- [~] Health and death (`EntityCitizen` hurt/die/checkHeal, `GraveManager`, `TileEntityGrave`): 20 health; a hit takes at most 20% (4), so it takes several to kill; stuck in a wall it is moved out instead; a player's hit of more than 1 does nothing without the HURT_CITIZEN permission (the mod's `EntityCitizen.checkIfValidDamageSource`; see Permissions); void and `/kill` take all its health, past the cap; hurt by a mob or player it runs 15 blocks away from it; hurt by anything else but a fall (a block, drowning, starving) it moves 5 blocks from where it stands, as in the mod (`EntityCitizen.performMoveAway`: `walkAwayFrom(blockPosition(), 5)`, skipped for falls); a hurt sleeper wakes, as vanilla does, then goes back to bed; it heals every 5 seconds unless a mob or player hurt it in the last 5: 2 when full, 1 normally, saturation/20/2 below 6. On death the colony's members who get colony messages (RECEIVE_MESSAGES) are told where and why, and a grave is put on the nearest free ground within 10 blocks with everything it carried (none in lava: items drop); the grave decays after 10 minutes and is gone 10 minutes later, dropping its items; the timer only runs while its chunk is loaded (the mod's grave ticks as a block entity)
  - Hits from mobs, players and arrows knock it back as vanilla would (strength 0.4, away from what hit it; not in bed, on a seat or on a ladder); an arrow that hits it is used up, as one that hits a player. Its body is collidable so arrows can hit it, on a no-collision team so nothing pushes it
  - Hut blocks and graves survive explosions
  - Breaking the Town Hall drops what every worker carried and what every grave holds
  - The hidden legs mob takes the hits (mobs that hunt villagers, like zombies, go for it); the hits themselves are cancelled
  - The sick heal only while under a third of their health, as the mod's. No calling guards for help; research adds health and faster healing (Research); only knights wear armour
  - Health shows in the town hall's citizen list
- [~] Graveyard and undertaker (`BuildingGraveyard`, `EntityAIWorkUndertaker`, `GraveManager`), the mod's numbers unless noted:
  - [x] The Graveyard hut (`mystic/graveyard`, 5 levels, default claims); recipe as the mod's (stone around a bone); unlocked by the Remembrance research, as in the mod
  - [x] The undertaker digs up the colony's graves one at a time (nearest the hut first; one undertaker per grave; ours: only graves in its world whose chunk is loaded), with a shovel from its hut chest (requested like other tools): empties it (up to 27 stacks a trip; more means a trip to the hut and back), tries to bring the citizen back, else digs the grave up and buries the citizen in a free burial spot of the graveyard (picked at random), then takes what the grave held to its hut. A decayed (mossy) grave is dug up the same as a fresh one; a grave that decays away completely or is broken meanwhile is given up. Called away (night, a meal, rain), it walks back and starts that step again
  - [x] Bringing back: 0.5% per hut level, at most 2.5% plus research (1% or 3%), still at most 2.5% (the mod's chance without totems or a mystical site), plus 0.125% a mana level; with Undertaker Emergency it runs on the way to a grave, not to bed or a meal (vanilla sprinting, +30%). The citizen comes back where the grave was with its name and skin, and its mourners stop mourning. Ours: as a new citizen otherwise (no job, home or happiness kept)
  - [x] Burial spots are the mod's named grave blocks in each level's blueprint: the converter puts a marker entity on each (with its facing) that the plugin reads, turned with the building. No free spot: "no room" and the body is lost, as in the mod. **Re-run `tools/convert_blueprints.py` for the spots to exist** in older converted packs
  - [x] Timings from the mod's effort counts: each swing (ours: every 5 ticks) adds its skill level (strength to empty and bury, mana to bring back) until 100, 400 and 400; at level 10, 2.5 s, 10 s and 10 s
  - [x] Hut menu: graves waiting, spots used, the chance, and who rests there. Advancements "Rest in Peace" (bury) and "Back From the Grave" (bring back), as the mod's citizen_bury and citizen_resurrect
  - Not done: totems of undying (research). Graves lasting longer and the undertaker running are research (Research)
- [~] Happiness (`CitizenHappinessHandler`, `HappinessConstants`, the mod's modifiers, weights and thresholds): 0 to 10, ten times the weighted mean of the factors that aren't neutral (1). Factors: school (weight 1; a pupil 2, a child with no school place 0), security (4: guards against everyone else, min(guards / (others × 2/3), 2), both counted from 1), social life (2: the share of citizens without a problem, each jobless adult, homeless or hungry citizen counting once per problem), food (3: from the last 10 foods eaten, kinds and good food against the house level), housing (3: 0 homeless, else house level / 3; × 0.75 after 7 days, × 0.5 after 14), work (2: 0.5 jobless, 2 at a hut above level 3; same 7/14 days), sleep (1.5: unhappy after 2 nights without lying down in a bed; guards never), got hurt (2: 0 for a day; not by fire or lightning), a citizen died (3: 0 for everyone for 3 days, not for a guard's death). Days count at nightfall. Shown in a citizen's own menu (right-click it), and in the Town Hall's Citizens page with the colony's average (5.5 with no citizens) and each factor averaged
  - Ours: the mod's "good food" is its own dishes; ours is any food that is made (cooked or crafted, like bread) rather than picked or caught
  - All factors neutral gives 10 (the mod divides by zero there); the mod's shared "death" modifier, which wears off faster the more citizens there are, is per citizen here
  - Its complaints (after 7 days homeless or jobless: on day 8, as the mod checks more than 7) and demands (after 14) are colony messages, not the mod's chat bubbles; no idle-at-job, mystical site or quest factors yet (sickness is under Sickness) ("beat a raid" is in), no happiness research
  - It sets new citizens' skill levels and how fast they learn (Skills), as the mod's
- [~] Sickness and the Hospital (`CitizenDiseaseHandler`, `EntityAISickTask`, `BuildingHospital`, `EntityAIWorkHealer`, the mod's diseases), the mod's numbers unless noted:
  - Diseases: influenza (rarity 100; cured by a carrot and a potato), measles (10; a poppy, a dandelion and kelp), smallpox (1; a honey bottle and a golden apple)
  - Falling sick: every 3 s, 10 x its modifier in 5 x 33333 (the mod's default disease setting 5; ours: not a setting), so about once in 14 hours for an ordinary citizen; the modifier is its job's (miners and swineherds 2, the crusher 4 with no skills, healers never), halved by a varied diet and up to 1.25x for a dull one once it has eaten 10 meals (0.5 x min(2.5, 5 / kinds)). Not while the colony has no more than its 4 starting citizens, nor while immune. Bumping into a sick citizen passes it on 1 time in 100 (ours: checked every 3 s with the chance of 60 bumps, bodies within 0.6 blocks); Masks stops half of those, and a stopped one gives 30 s of immunity (60 s with Vaccines)
  - Being sick: it stops working (guards when not fighting), heals only while under a third of its health (the last heal may go a little past it), and the Health happiness factor (weight 2) is 0.5, then x0.5 after 7 days sick and x0.1 after 14 (complaint and demand messages on days 8 and 15). With one of each cure item in its own inventory it holds them for a minute and is well, at full health, using one of each up. With no Hospital it wanders near its work; its status ("Sick: influenza", in green while it is sick) and its menu say what it needs (the mod's chat bubble, which a vanilla client can't show). With one it goes there, lies in a free bed of the building (ours: beds found from 4 below to 12 above the hut block; the sick there stay through raids and nights) and waits; 1 in 7200 each second, in a bed or waiting for one, it gets well by itself at full health. Badly hurt citizens (under 6 health, fed, not guards) go to a Hospital too, if there is one
  - Well again: immune for 90 minutes after a hospital bed (x10 with Vaccines), 45 minutes otherwise
  - Players: right-clicking a sick citizen with a golden apple cures it 1 time in 3; poisonous food (a poisonous potato, raw chicken, a spider eye, rotten flesh) makes a citizen sick; both use the item up whether or not they work, and the citizen takes nothing more for 5 minutes after an apple, 20 s after poison. Ours: sneak to open its menu instead, and a message when poison does nothing. The citizen menu shows the disease and its cure
  - Hospital (`fundamentals/hospital`, unlocked by Stamina; recipe as the mod's: a carrot in the middle): its healer, once a second, sees to its patients (the sick inside its building or in its beds): 2 in 3600 it cures one outright (hearts for 30 s); else it brings a patient one of each cure item from the hut chest into its inventory (it asks for 16 of each its patients lack, minimum 1); it heals players inside the Hospital more than 10 (and twice its level) below full to 5 (and its level) short of full, and walks to a citizen under 10 health within 40 blocks per hut level (across the ground) (a random one each second) to heal it the same way. Healers never fall sick
  - Ours: the healer keeps 16 of each cure item; when a patient's inventory is full, or it gets well before the healer arrives, the healer keeps the cure items for the next patient (with a colony message when full); hurt citizens at the Hospital are healed by the healer's walks rather than as patients. Its knowledge makes outright cures likelier (Skills). Not done: the citizen's food interactions besides poison (feeding, books for children, cactus), and the mod's statistics
- [~] Mourning (`CitizenMournHandler`, `EntityAIMournCitizen`): when a citizen other than a guard dies, those who shared its home (not guards or undertakers) mourn it the whole next day, from one morning to the next (someone who dies while they mourn gets a day of their own after): they don't work and wander near its named grave if the undertaker buried it, else near the town hall. ## Custom blocks and items

Everything the mod registers (`apiimp/initializer/ModBlocksInitializer.java` and `ModItemsInitializer.java`). The build tool, scan tool and decorative blocks come from the mod's companion mods (Structurize, Domum Ornamentum), not MineColonies itself. A vanilla client can't show new blocks or items, so each needs a stand-in: a tagged vanilla block or item, later with a custom model from a resource pack.

- [~] Optional resource pack, each player's choice: MineCP's items always carry a `custom_model_data` string (`minecp:hut/<type>`, `minecp:rack`, `minecp:build_goggles`, `minecp:build_tool`) beside their names, lore and tags, which shows nothing without the pack. `tools/build_resourcepack.py` builds a private pack from your own MineColonies (and Structurize) jars that overrides the vanilla chest, bell, hay bale, stick and leather helmet definitions to show the mod's models for those strings (vanilla for everything else). The server doesn't know or care who has it
  - Placed huts and storage racks stay vanilla chests, named for what they are ("Builder's Hut", "Storage Rack"): their inventory title for everyone. The plugin sends that name to clients (a vanilla server never does) so the pack's OptiFine / Entity Model Features chest models (`optifine/cem/chest<n>.jem`, chosen by name in `chest.properties`, converted from the mod's block models) can show the mod's hut and rack; vanilla clients see a plain chest. The Town Hall (a bell) and fields (hay bales) have no such model
  - The converted models drop element rotations (a few tilted decorations) and face texture rotations; double racks use the vanilla model
